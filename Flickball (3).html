Player 1A / D move

Every 3 goals you score charges your ball's special. Each ball has a different one, picked on the next screen. Click a key button to change its key.

Flickball

1v1 ball football with 100 balls, master orbs and wild special moves.

P pause, M mute, F fullscreen

How to play

Player 1A / D move
W jump (up to 3 times)
S dive in the air
Q special
Player 2Left / Right move
Up jump (up to 3 times)
Down dive in the air
Right Shift special

Score goals to charge your special. Grab orbs for boosts. Master orbs (glowing rings) trigger a cutscene and a homing shot. Specials can be rebound on the menu.
